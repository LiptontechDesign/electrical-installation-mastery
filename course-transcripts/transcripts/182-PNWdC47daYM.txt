shield in place to help prevent contact with live parts if you remove that and look at the connections you'll be able to see that they're a cage type terminal making for really easy connections that minimizes damage to the conductors and give a really secure termination the isolator up here is an ac23 type which means that it can be used for switching off highly inductive loads perfect for things like motors transformers and welders there's a couple of other great safety features on the outlet if we close the lid back up and have a look at the socket outlet we get another application of ip ratings because the terminals themselves are rated at ip2x which if you've completed our cpd package on this subject you'll know means that the terminals cannot be accessed by solids greater than 12 and a half millimetres in diameter or fingers as we call them the outlet goes one step further in this regard you can see that when i try to operate the isolating switch at the top it simply won't turn on this is because the socket is interlocked which means that it can't be energized without a plug being inserted which is a requirement in BS 7671 for certain types of installation so when we insert a plug into the outlet you can see if we just do that that it then allows for the operation of the isolation switch so there you go and you'll notice that once it's energized i can't then and manually operate the isolator switch you can see this long connected bar down here is trying to push downwards but it can't because of the molded aluminium section here looking at the socket element there's this plunger with a spring attached to it and when the plug connects it pushes it down and activates this lever which lifts up the bottom section of the sliding bar and then allows the whole bar to slide down when the isolator is operated you'll also see that when the plug goes in and the isolator switched it brings up this retaining hook which latches over the keyway on the plug and prevents the plug from being removed when the socket is energized another interesting requirement of IEC 60309 which relates to this kind of socket outlet is that the electrical contacts in the socket barrel are located at different depths to ensure that when inserting a plug the earth is connected first and then the neutral and then the phases when withdrawing the plug this is of course the opposite so the phases disconnect first then the neutral then the earth this sequence prevents loss of neutron three-phase systems which may be connected to single phase loads further down the line so
