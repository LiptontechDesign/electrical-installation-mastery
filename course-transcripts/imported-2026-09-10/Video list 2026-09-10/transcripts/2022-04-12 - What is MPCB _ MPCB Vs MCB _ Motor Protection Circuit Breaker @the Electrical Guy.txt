all know that full form of mcb is miniature circuit breaker and for mpcb is motor protection circuit breaker this mcb manufactured by cnc company has a rating of c40 this means if we try to flow current of magnitude 40 ampere then this mcb will trip maximum 40 ampere current can flow from this mcb but if i come to mpcb current rating is different here directly 40 ampere is written but here 4 to 6.3 ampere is written this means current rating is from 4 to 6.3 ampere rating for mcb is fixed but for mpcb we can change this rating you can set this rating depending upon your need you can see two buttons in mpcb with this button you can switch on and with another button you can switch off but this mcb is switch on or off in this way if you want to check your mcb whether working properly or not then there is no way if you push this upwards mcb will turn on and on pushing downwards it will turn off but if you want to check this mpcb then there is a way if you look closely to this mpcb test is written here below the test a slide button is present when you slide this button this mpcb will trip you just saw that our mpcb got tripped so we can slide this button to check whether mpcb is working properly or not this mcb provide protection from overload and short circuit if you try to flow 40 ampere current through this mcb then this mcb will trip if somehow both the phase wires bring short circuit then also this mcb will trip similarly this mpcb also provides protection from overload and short circuit along with these two mpcb also provide face loss protection let us understand what is face loss protection suppose i provided input power supply to this mpcb and similarly at output terminal i connected motor wires now always a three phase power should be supplied to motor and if there is a break in phase supply among the three our motor should be stopped if you use mcb in this condition it will not trip but if you supply three phase power supply to mpcb and suddenly one of the three phase power supply fail then if the mpcb is in on condition it will trip after this mpcb also provides current unbalanced protection this means the load connected at output terminal of mpcb will receive different current in these spaces like in our phase 10 ampere current is flowing y phase 2 ampere current is flowing and in b phase 10 ampere current is flowing then this mpcb will disconnect the power supply because in y phase 2 ampere current is flowing in another model of mpcb you will find no nc contact by using these contacts you can make on indication of indication as well as trip indication this means you can use no nc contact in any way as per your need friends we use mpcb to run motor with protection but this mcb can be used anywhere like in houses for motor for lighting for pumps wherever you want to use you can connect this mcb but this mpcb is used only for the purpose to run the motor with protection friends i hope you learned something new in this video
