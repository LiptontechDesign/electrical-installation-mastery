# Solar PV: a separate T1 → T2 → T3 study pathway

Integrated 27 October 2026 from the previously selected 26-video shortlist. Choose **Solar · T1–T3** in the course map or public curriculum. Three independently numbered modules contain nine sections and 4:45:36 of video. The public route, search, progress, editor destinations and Organise mode all recognise Solar. Existing C2, C1 and Advanced modules retain their identities, order and content.

## Learning order

| Module | Sections | Videos | Running time |

| T3 · Grid-Connected and Hybrid Systems | Domestic planning; Sam's Solar Journey; optional larger-system architecture | 16 | 2:37:50 |

T1 now opens with Paul Evans's PV-system explanation, before Joe Hammond's consumption example. Connector workmanship follows component understanding. Glen Morris's worked energy-sizing example opens T2; Nate Yarbrough's controller selection then leads into his disconnect, wiring and programming sequence. The two eFIXX testing/fault-investigation videos remain paired.

T3 starts with Joe Hammond's connected array/inverter/battery/siting block. Spirit Energy's introduction and eleven project episodes follow in the [publisher's original order](https://www.spiritenergy.co.uk/sams-solar-journey-video-kb), from layout and survey through installation and handover. The optional introductory trailer stays with that project. The 69:32 Fronius/Victron webinar is optional further study at the end, rather than a long prerequisite before the practical project. It teaches larger AC/DC-coupled architecture using historical product examples.

The controller videos are US mobile-system demonstrations, included because they clearly show equipment connections and configuration. Glen Morris supplies an Australian stand-alone sizing example. UK teaching remains the principal source for foundations, domestic planning, testing and the complete project case study. These contexts and their limits are preserved in the [source review](solar-source-review.md).

## Language, review and coverage

All 26 public player responses were available and permitted embedding on 27 October. Eighteen exposed an explicit English original audio-track label. Eight exposed a single audio stream, original English ASR, and English teaching from the credited publisher. The selection does not depend on a Hindi-original dub. YouTube controls the selected audio; the site cannot guarantee a language using the documented iframe API. English subtitles and the `hl` interface parameter are not substitutes for spoken audio.

The previous complete caption review is retained: 21 English exports and five Spirit exports read through Bengali/Arabic captions with translation. Those five now also expose English-original audio metadata; their earlier reviews are still honestly labelled translated-caption reviews. The [per-video ledger](solar-video-review.json) preserves hashes, ranges, language qualifications, source titles, current durations and availability. No complete independent listening to every second or visual electrical-design approval is claimed. New source transcripts remain in ignored research files.

The [MMU training outline](https://www.mmu.ac.ke/wp-content/uploads/dlm_uploads/2020/01/Renewable-Energy-short-Courses.pdf) and [University of Nairobi description](https://physics.uonbi.ac.ke/sites/physics.uonbi.ac.ke/files/2024-10/Solar%20academy%20Brochure-updated.pdf) inform the topic progression. MMU teaches T1/T2 together and separates T3 grid-tie study. Our T1/T2 boundary is a learning design, not an official examination blueprint or a statement of licence work limits. Large equipment examples do not establish T2 entitlement to install them. UK grid rules, US mobile-system arrangements and Australian design assumptions do not establish Kenyan compliance.

| Competency area | Reviewed selection and remaining work |
|---|---|
| Components, energy and terminations | Introductory teaching and connector demonstration; battery chemistry/care and resource assessment need more study and supervised practice. |
| Stand-alone energy sizing | A complete worked example; inverter W/VA, power factor and surge demand still need a separate full numerical exercise. |
| Cable/protection/earthing design | Demonstrated in parts; a complete Kenya-applicable design and practical assessment are still required. |
| Controller installation/settings | Connected equipment demonstrations; actual ratings and commissioning order require current manufacturer instructions. |
| Roof installation | A UK slate-roof case study; structural design, waterproofing and other roof types need specific teaching and supervised work. |
| Testing and faults | Paired measured checks and fault investigation; not every installation's complete commissioning schedule. |
| Grid and hybrid integration | Planning, architecture and a complete project narrative; detailed three-phase design, Kenyan grid procedures, anti-islanding and backup earthing/neutral coordination need further verified teaching and practice. |

This is a reviewed video study pathway, not a claim of complete EPRA examination coverage or practical competence. The learning interface has no new notes, technical asides or exercise panels. Existing Practice and Papers retain their C2/C1 source scopes.
