# Power-factor correction and APFC review

The existing C1 power-factor module follows Motor Starting, Protection and Speed Control. The existing module and section IDs, videos, private ordering and progress remain stable. Four optional videos were added as separate sections. Existing motor-specific capacitor precautions remain after the drive lessons.

Sources were checked on 30–31 October 2026; availability and dubbed tracks can change. Each selected video's complete available English auto-caption track was reviewed. Auto-caption errors and translated audio are not an authoritative wiring specification.

- [Capacitor-bank sizing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-kyppHRdwX4), Electrical Platform, 8:25: original English audio. Sizing and steps precede automatic operation.
- [APFC148 terminals and programming](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-7yilF7U6i4), Selec Controls, 6:58: English auto captions, English original spoken presentation. The manufacturer identifies inputs and outputs, then concentrates on settings; it is not a complete end-to-end wiring demonstration.
- [APFC panel wiring](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5084gua6bzY), Electrician Ustaad, 9:51: Hindi original, YouTube English auto-dub listed as available at review. The English auto-caption export covers the demonstration. Embedded players may not offer the same track selection as YouTube.
- [Control diagram and practical panel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pyW7vbB3rP0), Electrical Infinity, 13:23: English speech and English auto captions. The creator's updated companion to the user's 61m2l-moltc video traces auto/manual control wiring with a physical panel. It is primarily a control-circuit walkthrough, not comprehensive commissioning or capacitor power wiring.

The user's requested 61m2l-moltc and UbhEL_yG57k did not expose English audio-track metadata or captions during review, so English teaching could not be verified. The latter credits background music. Neither was added. q2LHmZ6NbEY exposed Hindi captions and no English audio track and was not added. Absence of a listed dub does not establish the reason or whether YouTube will add one later.

Source limitations: the sizing video overstates the effects of low power factor and presents fixed compensation at light load too broadly; fixed compensation can overcorrect when load changes. The Electrician Ustaad demonstration uses a plywood mockup and presents its controller's phase arrangement too generally; actual panel construction, controller sensing, CT polarity and phase connections depend on the equipment and design. The Electrical Infinity video describes a particular panel and control circuit, not a universal wiring standard. Use the actual controller and capacitor manufacturer's documentation, applicable local rules and supervised practical instruction. Capacitors retain charge and CT secondary circuits require proper handling.

All prior section membership is unchanged. Existing personal copies of the added media remain eligible for preservation as separate occurrences. The update does not alter curriculum revision 14 or create new learner-facing lesson notes.
