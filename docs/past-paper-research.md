# Past-paper study compilation: source and technical audit

Reviewed 15 October 2026.

## Scope and editorial contract

The user explicitly requested separate C1 and C2 tabs after Practice, consolidated questions with comprehensive beginner-friendly answers, question breakdowns and professional LaTeX calculations. The user also requested that source labels, discussions of other answer keys and repetitive correction commentary remain out of the student view.

The research located eight dated C1 archive copies and two dated C2 copies. These are third-party uploads, not an independently authenticated EPRA archive. The search cannot establish that every historical paper has been found. Some copies have incomplete pages or unreadable figures. An undated revision sheet is not counted as a past paper.

The resulting bank contains 87 independently written questions: 78 in C1 and 24 in C2, with shared recurring concepts consolidated. These are related study exercises, not verbatim transcriptions, official examination papers or official marking schemes. The cross-reference register records topic relationships, not claimed official examiner intent or exact one-to-one equivalence. Where the source was ambiguous, a well-posed teaching version states its own assumptions and givens.

Original papers and entire standards are not redistributed. SVG schematics and answers are newly authored. Linked BS definitions come from the existing user-supplied historical 17th-edition library; the expandable label identifies that edition. New term explanations are paraphrases. The supplied content/epra archive, source slices and eight authorised display omissions are unchanged.

## Independent technical decisions

- Single-phase 200 V, 1000 W input, PF 0.7 to unity, 50 Hz: 1020.204 var and 81.1853 μF (81.19 μF), not 79.6 μF. The 400 V shaft-output case uses efficiency 0.84 and the stated PF target 0.9, yielding 126.917 μF.
- 100 kW electrical input, 415 V, PF 0.8 to 0.97: 49.9376 kVAr, initial current 173.901 A and corrected source current 143.423 A. A 63 A motor protective rating is incompatible with this input. Motor and capacitor branch duties remain separate.
- AHU 415 V, 120 A, PF 0.7 to 0.95: P 60.3793 kW, Q1 61.5992 kVAr, Q2 19.8457 kVAr and compensation 41.7535 kVAr.
- Measured transformer apparent demand comes from measured current, not nameplate capacity: 850 A at 415 V yields 610.981 kVA; 800 A yields 575.041 kVA. Demand-charge calculations use explicitly given exercise rates, not claimed current tariffs.
- Four poles at 50 Hz have synchronous speed 1500 rpm. At ideal zero slip the shaft speed is 1500 rpm; 1800 rpm belongs to four poles at 60 Hz. Actual no-load induction slip is generally small rather than exactly zero.
- Generator 100 kVA, 415 V: 139.1205 A. Factor 0.91 gives a load-based table target 152.8797 A. A hypothetical 154 A table row gives only 140.14 A corrected capacity, not support for a 160 A protective setting. No missing source cable table or 50 mm² selection is invented.
- Office wording is ambiguous about lamp versus fitting ratings and ring diversity. The study version explicitly states per-lamp losses, full 32 A ring loading, phase balance and no diversity; its full connected-load result is 462.963 A. It does not claim an official diversified main-switch answer.
- Unreadable bills are replaced with a clearly specified arithmetic exercise, not reconstructed invoice totals. Transformer OCR ambiguities are replaced by explicit teaching data (single phase, 250 kVA, 11 kV/415 V, 50 Hz, 80 secondary turns).
- A source building says two storeys but names ground, first and second; the authored question states all three levels explicitly. The original missing C2 floor plan is not reconstructed; the layout question teaches the method and the switching diagram is an authored example.
- A delta winding arrangement has no ordinary neutral terminal; a neutral-current calculation is attached to the actual star/four-wire arrangement.
- Transformer maximum efficiency is a loss-balance condition, not a universal full-/half-load percentage. Absolute Lorenz resistance determination is distinguished from comparative Wheatstone-bridge measurement.
- Electrode, Ze and Zs acceptance depend on the actual protection and disconnection arrangement; arbitrary universal 10 Ω or 200 Ω pass/fail rules are avoided. CPC, earthing and bonding functions are distinguished; no unauthorised consumer N–PE bridge is proposed.
- Ordinary up-to-500 V non-SELV/PELV insulation testing: usual BS criterion 500 V DC / 1 MΩ, with the specified reduced-voltage conditions distinguished. Unperformed CPC continuity and 0.12 MΩ readings cannot be declared satisfactory.
- Bathroom guidance uses the currently published 2.5 m horizontal separation from the zone-1 boundary, with its specified exceptions, rather than silently retaining a historical 3 m option.
- First aid follows adult BLS 2025: prompt local emergency call, assessment of breathing, adult compressions 100–120/min at 5–6 cm, 30:2 for trained rescuers able to give breaths, AED prompts. Obsolete back-pressure/arm-lift methods are not taught.
- Four-quadrant VFD operation requires the actual drive and energy-handling arrangement; a generic diode front end is not assumed to regenerate to mains. Star-delta requires a suitable delta-running motor, six terminals and a safe interlocked transition.
