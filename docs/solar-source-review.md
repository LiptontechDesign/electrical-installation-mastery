# Solar video selection and source review

Selection reviewed 26 October 2026; integration and original-English audio evidence rechecked 27 October. The selected 26 videos are now in the separate Solar pathway. See [the integrated sequence and coverage map](solar-pathway.md) and [current evidence](solar-video-review.json). The source qualifications below remain applicable.

## Review scope

- 26 selected public videos: complete available caption text read.
- 21 exported English captions. Five Spirit exports were Bengali/Arabic and were read with translation; these are not counted as English-transcript reviews.
- English teaching is supported by channel/publisher context and English caption-track metadata where available. Continuous independent listening to every second of original audio is not claimed.
- Automatic captions can confuse units, names and technical words. They do not certify the correctness of every physical action shown. A full visual inspection of every video is not claimed.
- Original creator order is preserved for Joe's selected sequence, Nate's disconnect/wiring/programming block, and the complete Spirit case study. Placing separate teaching blocks under T1/T2/T3 is our learning design.
- Current Kenyan training outlines were checked. A current official solar examination blueprint, question weighting and all expected examination tasks were not verified; no pass or complete-coverage claim is made.

## Why the shortlist changed

Glen Morris's energy-sizing lesson provides a connected calculation with explicit losses and a design-month resource. It is used for that purpose. The Australian location, voltages, autonomy choices and standards are case assumptions, not Kenyan defaults.

His separate maximum-demand lesson (hAoR6QFjfOI) was inspected and excluded from the selected core. It describes power factor 0.9 as 10% reactive power and uses misleading negative-power-factor language. For sinusoidal conditions, PF = P/S; reactive power is not (1-PF) times the apparent power. The useful measuring discussion does not make the whole lesson suitable as an unqualified exam source. This rejected video was not reviewed completely.

The five-part altE design sequence was reviewed through its available English captions. It is coherent but was excluded from the main route. Step 1 treats AC volts × amps as watts without accounting for power factor. Step 2's example of 81% available capacity uses 1.19 rather than the reciprocal 1/0.81, approximately 1.235. Later steps use US NEC factors and nominal-voltage shortcuts. These require too much qualification to make this the main Kenyan beginner calculation sequence. No claim is made of a complete read of the separate altE components series.

The 100 W Footprint Hero kit lesson (MZ_vRUknhzw) was read completely and excluded. It deliberately omits fusing and shows a large inverter connected with clips. That does not provide the protected practical model sought here.

The eFIXX electrical-preparation lesson (fMM-EPRKNhw) was reviewed in the earlier research and excluded from this core. Inverter insulation monitoring is not, by itself, array overcurrent protection. The first four planning lessons have narrower teaching roles; selecting them does not endorse every claim elsewhere in the course.

EXPLORIST's array-fusing lesson (s1P31hxlD3I) was read completely and excluded. Its closing diode explanation can be read as treating bypass diodes as blocking reverse current. The reverse-current and fuse-design method must follow the actual system and applicable requirements. The retained practical videos demonstrate specific assembly/configuration work; they are not the complete protection design.

UK Electrical SIM's off-grid series was investigated. Several complete transcripts were read, but not the whole series; no complete-series approval is claimed. The simulated system omits some real protection and simplifies battery behaviour. It is not selected as evidence of physical installation competence.

BluePower Pro's ESS lesson (ofg7a3L7Uec) was read completely. It contains useful architecture and regional discussion, but it was not added alongside the selected manufacturer webinar. Detailed regional settings and some explanatory generalisations would need further qualification.

Mark Allison's two installation videos were inspected but not approved for this core. The review was not a complete read of both. The instructor explicitly presents parts as his learning journey, including an unresolved termination choice; this is not the strongest beginner model for the requested purpose.

## Qualifications on retained material

- The Engineering Mindset lesson is an introduction, with simplified cell physics and older illustrative efficiency ranges. Backup during a grid outage depends on appropriate islanding and backup equipment; a battery alone does not establish this capability.
- Joe Hammond's lessons provide a connected domestic planning example. UK irradiation, tariffs, connection procedures and product limits are not universal. Equipment nameplate power is not an absolute cap on PV output under every condition; clipping should not be treated as all available surplus energy necessarily being dissipated as inverter heat.
- The controller-sizing example assumes an all-series array. It does not replace checks of PV-input short-circuit current, hot-condition working voltage, all temperature limits and manufacturer permissions for overpanelling.
- Nate's practical lessons use specific mobile-system equipment and battery charging instructions. Battery configuration and commissioning must follow the current manuals; neither DC-negative bonding nor a disconnect rating is a universal fixed-installation prescription.
- eFIXX connector dimensions, torque values and test instruments concern the equipment shown. Manufacturer compatibility and installation instructions govern the actual work. The two testing videos form a useful failed-test/fault-investigation sequence; their example settings are not every system's complete test procedure.
- Spirit is a compact case study. Its 7.6% versus 5.3% shade-loss comparison is a 2.3 percentage-point reduction, not a universal 2.3% relative gain. Its cost comparisons and structural arrangement are project-specific.
- The 2020 Fronius/Victron webinar teaches architecture. Present-day product limits, firmware, battery compatibility, grid approval and configuration require current instructions. Microgrid settings are not general grid-connection settings.
