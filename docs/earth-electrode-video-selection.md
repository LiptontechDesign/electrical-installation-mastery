## The supplied Schneider page

Read [Installation and measurements of earth electrodes](https://www.electrical-installation.org/enwiki/Installation_and_measurements_of_earth_electrodes). Its descriptions of rings, rods/pipes and plates, soil conditions, seasonal changes and corrosion provide broader context.

The page describes two measurement arrangements:

- **Pairwise ammeter method:** measure the three electrode-pair resistances A, B and C; the electrode under test has resistance **(A + C − B) / 2**. This is a distinct method; the selected playlist does not demonstrate that exact three-pair procedure.
- **Direct-reading earth tester:** use current and potential stakes and repeated readings to establish a stable result. John Ward Part 3 demonstrates this arrangement.

The page uses an approximate 0.68 probe position, while common fall-of-potential explanations discuss 62%. Neither is a universal position for every electrode geometry. Adequate separation and confirmation of a stable result matter; use the tester's instructions and applicable test procedure.

Soil resistivity (ohm-metres), electrode resistance (ohms) and installation loop impedance (ohms) answer different questions. UK example values must not be presented as universal Kenyan acceptance limits. Kenyan requirements, approved designs and equipment instructions govern actual installations, as stated in the course's existing safety notice.
