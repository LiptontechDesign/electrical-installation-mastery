## Review evidence and limits

The creator metadata and matching embed URL were checked through YouTube oEmbed for all 28 selected videos. Earlier review used available English captions for 30 of the 33 videos across the two playlists, plus creator descriptions and primary references. Among the selected videos, Earthing & Bonding 2 lacked an accessible English transcript; Safe Isolation was assessed from its description because captions were disabled. These are not claims that every video was played in full or that automatic captions are error-free.

Durations come from available watch-page metadata, otherwise the playlist metadata. UK regulatory examples remain flagged as regulation-sensitive and must be interpreted using the course's existing Kenyan safety notice. No UK exam instructions were added to the C2 course.

References used in the review:

- [EPRA written and oral interview areas of competency](https://www.epra.go.ke/sites/default/files/2025-06/Written%20%26%20Oral%20Interviews%20Areas%20of%20Competency.pdf).
- [IET: resistance readings for step 3 of the ring final circuit test](https://electrical.theiet.org/wiring-matters/years/2022/89-march-2022/resistance-readings-for-step-3-of-the-ring-final-circuit-test/).
- [IET: EICRs, coding and the Electricity at Work Regulations webinar questions](https://electrical.theiet.org/bs-7671-18th-edition-wiring-regulations/faqs/webinar-questions-and-answers/eicrs-coding-and-the-link-to-the-electricity-at-work-regulations-1989-webinar/).

## Saved courses and exports

Missing published lessons are inserted before their next known core neighbour when a saved course is normalised. Existing learner ordering, moved lessons, supplementary placements, archived/deleted IDs, revisions and operation receipts are retained. Permanently removed lessons are not restored. Retired catalogue rows no longer appear in navigation; account progress and notes are not bulk-deleted.

Following the teaching update, the nine URL-only files in `C2 YouTube Links/` contain 213 catalogue URLs in course order, one file per C2 module. Personal supplementary additions are outside these canonical exports.
