# Comprehensive Solar Installation Training T1–T3

**Viewing order: 72 existing videos in 13 modules.** Titles are descriptive course labels; the creators, YouTube links and lesson identities remain unchanged. The six-step off-grid overview and the small-system demonstration were moved to the beginning; self-consumption lessons precede the hybrid gateway example. No new videos were added.

The prior review sampled passages across the supplied transcripts and read selected transcripts more closely; it was not a complete review of every transcript. Two videos lack captions and the off-grid installation video at the start of Module 11 has unreliable automatic captions. Its placement also uses the creator’s illustrated project. Video labels below do not imply an independent full technical audit.

## Before you begin

Review voltage, current, power, energy, polarity and series/parallel circuits in the existing electrical foundations. The early small-system video is a visual introduction. Its spark demonstration and fuse sizes are not a procedure to copy; follow the equipment instructions and qualified supervision.

[EDWartens course](https://edwartens.co.uk/student/courses/rooftop-solar-pv-design-installation) · [Open Green Energy project](https://www.opengreenenergy.com/diy-off-grid-solar-system-v2-0-2/)

## 1. Solar System Overview, Sunlight and PVGIS

Get an overview of solar system design and a small system, then study sunlight and PVGIS yield estimates.

### See a complete solar system before sizing it

1. [Plan an Off-Grid Solar System: Six Design Steps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CHYVhNdlYM4) — Maps the full off-grid design workflow from loads through the main system components.
2. [Small 100 W Solar Kit: Components and Connections](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uobUwjCLfok) — Introduces the component connections in a small kit. Observe the layout; do not copy the spark demonstration or assume its fuse sizes fit another system.

### Sunlight: irradiance, sun path and shading

3. [Solar Radiation, Sun Path and Shading Explained](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSv70imrPjs) — Solar intensity, sun path, air mass and shading explain the inputs used in the next videos.

### Use PVGIS to estimate sunlight and solar yield

4. [Use PVGIS to Estimate a Home’s Solar Generation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MdpQci4vTLU) — Start with a residential PVGIS walkthrough and compare monthly generation for a fixed array.
5. [Use PVGIS to Calculate a Solar Project’s Energy Yield](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=egReBSmaNCs) — Repeat the workflow at project scale and see how the horizon and assumptions affect the yield estimate.
6. [Download Solar Irradiance and Temperature Data from PVGIS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7OzKgvnYVs) — Export monthly and hourly resource data. This is the preparation for a standalone sizing exercise that depends on a weak solar month.
7. [UK PV Yield and Shading Estimate: An Older MCS Calculator](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=65IBdi0enrE) — Reference example of MCS-style yield and shading inputs in a 2013 calculator. Treat its interface and financial assumptions as historical.

## Further practice and remaining gaps

These videos do not by themselves complete a T1–T3 qualification or a verified design. Practise a load schedule, PV and battery sizing, a single-line diagram, cable and protection schedules, and a test and handover record under qualified supervision. The existing videos introduce protection, but additional worked device selection, fault-current and coordination practice is still needed.

For a solar-first system with utility backup and no export, compare an appropriate transfer-based arrangement with a grid-parallel hybrid using export control. For the equipment selected, verify operating modes, measurement, limits, switching, earthing, loss of supply and commissioning. The existing videos introduce parts of this topic; they do not form one complete no-export design. See Victron’s [system design](https://www.victronenergy.com/media/pg/Energy_Storage_System/en/system-design.html), [configuration](https://www.victronenergy.com/media/pg/Energy_Storage_System/en/configuration.html) and [commissioning](https://www.victronenergy.com/media/pg/Energy_Storage_System/en/commissioning.html) guidance.

The final two UK export-payment videos are optional background for a no-export project. UK connection and building-regulation examples describe UK procedures and should not be treated as Kenyan requirements. Advanced three-phase and commercial-system design, inspection and hands-on training remain separate study needs.
