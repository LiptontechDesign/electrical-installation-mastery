Module 7 uses Gary Hayers's [consumer-unit assembly demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=62lEhAuzeAI) and [the checks on that installation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dlkxzIQFvrk). It is a small training installation; the later supply-arrangement and isolation sections remain necessary, and full commissioning follows in Module 8.

Module 8 follows eight core lessons from [John Ward's Electrical Installation Testing playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLVsHvs2SuqmrHBQ0PDDHzq2LXkssRBp4Q). The separate phase-sequence lesson belongs to three-phase study and is outside this C2 opening section; the additional ring-resistance derivation is also omitted. These lessons combine explanations, diagrams and bench demonstrations. Existing visual-inspection, instrument setup, certification and complete installation walkthroughs remain in the following sections.

The [GSH RCD update](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zIPgYcLdQZE) appears immediately before John Ward's RCD demonstration. The older video is explicitly titled **Earlier Test Sequence**. Its half-current and five-times-current test sequence must not be read as the current minimum required schedule; see the [IET explanation of the changed BS 7671 RCD testing requirements](https://electrical.theiet.org/wiring-matters/years/2022/91-july-2022/changes-to-rcd-testing-in-bs-76712018plusa22022/). The later ring-final reinforcement section retains JPElectric's explanation of varying R1+R2 readings; see the [IET's ring-final step-3 guidance](https://electrical.theiet.org/wiring-matters/years/2022/89-march-2022/resistance-readings-for-step-3-of-the-ring-final-circuit-test/). Historical references in the videos still require the applicable current standards and installation requirements.

Module 9 follows Learn Electrics's genuine three-part sequence: [Part 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RY5YLgJjmd8), [Part 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IN5wIHoMtAo), and [Part 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Alz8iTv951k). These are narrated diagrams and worked fault-location examples. They introduce short-circuit and earth-fault tracing; the later lighting, open-circuit, insulation, borrowed-neutral and leakage lessons broaden the diagnostic coverage.
