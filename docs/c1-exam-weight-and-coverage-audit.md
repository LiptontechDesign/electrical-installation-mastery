# C1 written marks, oral priorities and course coverage

Reviewed 21 October 2026 against course commit `bfacafd`: 17 C1 modules, 98 sections and 169 mixed video rows. This is an evaluation of the present course, including the newly merged protective-device module. No lessons, learner records or exam questions are changed by this audit.

**Finding:** the course teaches the main recurring technical subjects well. It is not yet a completely self-contained video route for every high-mark written task or oral demonstration. The most valuable next improvements are complete worked building drawings, measured-load/PFC/billing calculations, whole-schedule test interpretation and Kenyan licensing/documentation. Electrode testing is available in C2 but is easy to miss when following only C1.

**Motor follow-up:** the subsequent [complete motor-caption audit](c1-motor-transcript-audit.md) supersedes the broad motor verdict below. It freshly reads all 30 motor-module videos and 12 related lessons, recovers May 2022 motor extracts and identifies incomplete star–delta control drawings, missing slip-ring resistance starting/synchronous hunting and other narrower application gaps. This general audit's original method did not include that full reread.

## Oral: what can be ranked honestly?

No authenticated C1 past oral paper with a topic-by-topic mark allocation was located. The official competency page covers both formats but gives **no oral topic marks**. Therefore a statement such as “earthing is the highest-mark oral question” is not supported by this review. Class B oral papers and solar T3 oral material are not C1 evidence. A third-party draft-guideline upload was also excluded as authority for current pass rules or compulsory oral weights.

The following is an **editorial preparation priority**, inferred from EPRA's competency scope and the practical demands of the written tasks:

1. **Isolate and prove safely, identify the instrument and explain its limitations.** Demonstrate the reasoning instead of reciting an order.
2. **Explain the earthing/fault path, perform or describe the test setup, interpret results and decide whether energisation/certification is justified.** Include electrodes, not just Zs.
3. **Identify a device and justify its application.** Distinguish switching from fault protection; explain poles, rating, trip mechanism, fault duty and coordination.
4. **Draw a building supply/riser and defend the cable/protection choices.** Trace phases, neutral and PE through floor boards and final circuits.
5. **Trace DOL/star–delta/reversing controls and diagnose a motor symptom.** Explain holding contacts, interlocks, overload operation and loss of supply.
6. **Explain the power triangle and compensation effect, then work a short example.** Distinguish PF from efficiency and load kW from nameplate capacity.
7. **State Kenyan licence boundaries and the purpose of local records.** Use verified current local sources; do not substitute a foreign example.

The current course supports the explanations in items 1–6, with the practical/application limits above. Item 7 needs a focused Kenyan bridge. This priority order is not an official examiner weighting, and no claim is made that an online video establishes hands-on ability.

## Improvement order justified by this audit

1. **Building-answer capstone:** produce one floor-by-floor single-line/riser from the brief; show circuit allocation, neutral/PE, devices, selection rationale and the planned verification record. Put the drawing/design portion after C1 **10**, before/with the applications in **11**; complete its commissioning portion after **14**.
2. **PFC and bill calculation capstone:** measured current → S/P/Q → fixed bank → new PF/kVA → surcharge/demand saving; also one shaft-output/efficiency-to-µF example. Place after the target-bank foundation in **6**, with bills as a clearly specified worked application.
3. **Full test-results decision exercise and oral rehearsal:** assess the complete mixed schedule, choose corrective action and retest before signing. Place after **14**, before periodic inspection **15**. Make the relevant C2 electrode lessons easy to revisit from C1 earthing/testing.
4. **Kenyan scope/documentation bridge:** licence boundaries, roles, documents and local supply responsibilities, based on confirmed primary sources. Introduce at entry to C1 and apply at handover.
5. **Narrow remaining variants:** single-wattmeter unbalanced measurement, conventional lightning schematic and complete motor diagnosis. Prefer focused worked explanations to another broad overview.

These are recommendations from the requested evaluation, not unreviewed video additions. No candidate is approved without the user's established transcript-and-flow review standard.
